STEPS.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
Relief from the exhastion he was feeling did not come as he had expected: now lying in bed; blinking eyes still unaccustomed to the fresh darkness of his room. His mind races as he worries he's forgotten something, repeating the checklist to no end. Eyes closing now he finds not the void of coming rest, but the slowly morphing noise of the black behind his eyelids. He thinks of how his eyes will never stop seeing and that simply covernig them doesn't seem to work anymore. Opening his eyes when the patterns begin to make him dizzy and closing them again when he's recovered, he eventually welcomes color into the darkness. Words from earlier in the day ring in the ears as the faces of those who spoke them appear piece by piece as if assembling a puzzle. He feels his body, still in the quiet dark, slipping away, but welcomes this with the expectation of a more energetic tomorrow. His head falls heavy until a noise shakes him, His eyes snap open, his heart racing; he pauses to listen to an unexpected all consuming silence. The once pure black of shadow now is broken into the fuzzy forms of his room: The side table, the dresser, the desk and the chair; he looks quietly past these though as his eyes fall on the door cornered opposite his bed. He listens. What other sound could have woken him with such a start? He knew what it had to be and yet its sudden silence surely couldn't be. Another silent moment as his excitement fades back into exhaustion and he lets his eyes fall closed. He finds himself following the same steps he had previously, though taking with each a little less time than before, until he felt his body begin to grow heavy once again when also repeated came the noise and his shocked alertness. He grabs hold of his head as he throws the blanket off himself and swings his legs over the edge of the bed where his feet rest on the floor. It's in this position he realizes that once again: silence reigns. He stands and enters the hall; passing the bathroom on his right, he leans into the bedroom on his left. He crosses the room-- avoiding the mess of earlier in the day--and leans over the crib. Still asleep, he thought, he must have made the sounds up. He is, he grants himself, exhausted. Turning around, he stumbles into the hall, past the bathroom, now on his left, and returns to his bedroom, drawing his blankets over himself and beginning the process once again.
