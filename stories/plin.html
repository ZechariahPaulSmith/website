PLIN.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
Plin feels as though her arms get in the way of her sleep. Nightly she lies awake, exhausted, imagining what wonderful dreams she might never experience due to her uncompromising limbs.

Old haunts draw her attention at this hour; the many times her words failed her emotions and betrayed her--much like her arms now, she thinks. Some of this time she takes to finish past arguments and solve the world's problems: it's all so obvious, why can't everyone else see?

Maybe she should read, she thinks, the book she's been meaning to read; the book she wants to read: it's right there, waiting on the bed-side table. No. She's too tired, she decides. She knows she'll forget every word.

She doesn't know why she goes through this every night, but she's never known any other way.
----------------------------------------------------------------------
